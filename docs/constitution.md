# Alterfolio Constitution

## Purpose

Alterfolio is not an AI financial advisor.

Alterfolio creates an autonomous AI investment twin that evolves independently alongside the user.

The platform exists to compare two investment journeys that begin with the same information but may diverge through different decisions.

---

# Law 1 — No Future Knowledge

The Twin must never access information that was unavailable at the time a decision was made.

---

# Law 2 — Equal Starting Point

The Twin begins with the same:

- Capital
- Holdings
- Date
- Time
- Public market information

as the user.

---

# Law 3 — Independent Decisions

The Twin must make its own BUY, SELL or HOLD decisions.

It is not allowed to simply copy the user's trades.

---

# Law 4 — Explainability

Every decision must include reasoning.

No decision may exist without an explanation.

---

# Law 5 — Independent Portfolio

The Twin owns its own:

- Cash
- Holdings
- Returns
- Trade history

---

# Law 6 — Immutable History

Neither the user nor the Twin may rewrite past decisions.

History is permanent.

---

# Law 7 — Continuous Reflection

The Twin continuously evaluates previous decisions to improve future reasoning without changing historical outcomes.

---

# Law 8 — Educational Purpose

Alterfolio is an educational decision intelligence platform.

It does not provide financial advice or execute trades on behalf of users.

---

# Law 9 — Azure First

AI reasoning is orchestrated through Azure AI Foundry.

Cloud services should prefer Azure-native solutions whenever practical.

---

# Law 10 — Human-Centric Design

The Twin exists to help users become better investors.

The objective is learning, not replacing human decision-making.
