# Alterfolio

## Vision

Alterfolio is an Azure AI Foundry-powered AI Twin platform that creates an alternative investment timeline alongside the user. The AI starts with the same portfolio, cash, and publicly available market information, independently makes investment decisions, and continuously compares performance, reasoning, and risk to help users become better investors.

## Problem Statement

Retail investors know the outcome of the decisions they made.
They never know the outcome of the decisions they didn't make.

Alterfolio solves this by maintaining an AI investment twin that evolves alongside the user.

## MVP Goal

Answer one question:

"Would my AI twin have made a better investment decision than I did?"

## Success Criteria

- User can record trades.
- Portfolio is automatically reconstructed.
- AI twin maintains its own portfolio.
- User can compare both portfolios.
- AI explains every decision.
